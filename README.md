
# Global EV Market Analysis 🚗⚡

## Project Overview
Global EV Market Analysis is a data analytics and visualization project focused on exploring trends and patterns in the Electric Vehicle (EV) industry.

The project uses Tableau to analyse electric vehicles across 15 different vehicle segments using a dataset of 100,000 vehicles.

## Objectives
- Analyse electric vehicle pricing across different vehicle categories.
- Compare driving ranges and battery technologies.
- Understand the relationship between vehicle price and driving range.
- Analyse drivetrain performance (AWD, FWD and RWD).
- Explore sales distribution across different countries.
- Understand customer ratings and vehicle performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau Public:** Data visualization, dashboards and storytelling.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Dataset handling and preparation.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and version control.
- **Netlify:** Website hosting and deployment.

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains 100,000 electric vehicle records with 37 attributes covering:
- Vehicle specifications
- Battery capacity and battery types
- Driving range
- Vehicle pricing
- Charging information
- Drivetrain specifications
- Country-wise sales
- Customer ratings

*Note: The dataset is synthetic and is used for analytical and educational purposes.*

## Key Findings
1. Electric scooters have the lowest cost per kilometre of driving range.
2. Price and driving range show a weak positive correlation of approximately 0.28.
3. AWD vehicles demonstrate higher average power compared to FWD and RWD vehicles.
4. NMC and LFP are the most common battery types in the dataset.
5. Average driving range is relatively similar across different battery types.
6. China records the highest sales in the dataset.
